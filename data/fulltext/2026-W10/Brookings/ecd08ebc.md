# What’s next for the Iran war? Experts discuss.

**來源：** Brookings
**日期：** 2026-03-09
**URL：** https://www.brookings.edu/articles/whats-next-for-the-iran-war-experts-discuss/

---

This week, Brookings experts convened to discuss the Iran war and its implications for Iran, the Middle East, and international security. This episode of The Current offers highlights from that event, moderated by Michael O’Hanlon, and including panelists Philip Gordon, Mara Karlin, Jeff Feltman, and Suzanne Maloney.

MALONEY: Because I think first and foremost, we’ve got to be really clear in articulating publicly to the American people, but also to our partners and allies in the region about precisely what it is we’re trying to accomplish. I think the other important point is that we are going to be dealing with some version of this regime in the aftermath of this conflict. We can’t go back to status quo ante. The priority for the aftermath of this conflict has to be creating the incentive structure and that includes both the possibilities of openings, but also the real continuation of penalties to try to ensure that whomever is in charge in Tehran, in the Islamic Republic, when this all ends is going to be incentivized to build a different future for the Iranian people.
It’s possible that there are people within the system who in the past were sympathetic to at least some degree of reform. We are going to have to make that a top priority, not just an ancillary interest for the United States.
