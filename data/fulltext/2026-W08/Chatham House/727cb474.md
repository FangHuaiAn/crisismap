Dr Sanam Vakil is Director of the Middle East and North Africa Programme at Chatham House.
Our Middle East and North Africa programme undertakes research and offers expert analysis on overlooked and underestimated issues in the region. Our methodology provides new frameworks for understanding on-the-ground realities and related policy implications.
Follow us on X (formerly Twitter), Facebook and LinkedIn, and visit our digital platform Kalam for analysis and commentary in English and Arabic.
Activities
- Carrying out field research and data collection;
- Publishing policy research, commentary and analysis in English and Arabic;
- Convening discussions with decision-makers and experts in policy, academia, civil society, and the private sector;
- Providing briefings to decision-makers and contributing to policy forums in the MENA region, the UK, the US and beyond;
- Contributing analysis to media coverage of the MENA region.
Research areas
- Geopolitical dynamics and the role of regional and international actors;
- Drivers and effects of transnational conflict;
- Interconnected political economic networks and their impact on conflict;
- Governance and accountability;
- Evolving state-society relations and citizen mobilization.
