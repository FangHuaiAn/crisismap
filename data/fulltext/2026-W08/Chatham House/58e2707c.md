# War in Ukraine

**來源：** Chatham House
**日期：** 2026-02-22
**URL：** https://www.chathamhouse.org/regions/russia-and-eurasia/ukraine?trending=

---

The ongoing Russian invasion is destroying a country which was already struggling with complex and fluid internal dynamics as a result of ongoing issues with the system of old rules and vested interests.
There is a strong demand and pressure from society and a new generation of policymakers for new rules, and the international support being provided to Ukraine to defend itself against Russia means a new future is inevitable.
Ukraine has been a major focus for Chatham House research for many years, long before the current Russian invasion, with our Ukraine Forum providing a unique platform for insight on the country’s internal dynamics in key policy areas, as well as its international relations.
