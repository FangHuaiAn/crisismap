Creon Butler leads the Global Economy and Finance Programme at Chatham House.
The Global Economy and Finance programme maintains links with policymakers, practitioners and researchers around the globe. We work to ensure that independent analysis of global economic issues translates into practical and timely policy insights and recommendations.
Activities
- Undertaking original policy research on cutting edge issues affecting the global economy and finance;
- Convening round table discussions, seminars and workshops, bringing together the work of leading scholars, policymakers and business representatives;
- Holding private briefings with policymakers, business leaders, policy officials and international authorities.
Research focus
- International economic cooperation;
- Global economic governance and the role of the G7 and G20;
- Economics of climate change;
- International trade and investment;
- Developing country debt;
- Innovative finance for global health;
- Corporate purpose and responsibilities;
- Evolution of the international monetary system.
